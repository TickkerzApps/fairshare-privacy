Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 27, 2026
The short version: PaycheckPro stores all your data locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.

1. Overview

PaycheckPro ("the App") is a paycheck calculator developed by Tickkerz. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with your use of PaycheckPro on Android devices.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. PaycheckPro is designed to work entirely offline — your data never leaves your device.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

PaycheckPro does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Names or personal identifiers
  • Financial information, income data, or salary figures
  • Tax filing status or withholding details
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Crash reports sent to external servers
  • Any other personal or sensitive data

3. How Your Data Is Stored

All data you enter into PaycheckPro — including pay rates, schedules, deductions, and tax settings — is stored exclusively in your device's local storage using Android's standard AsyncStorage mechanism.

This data is:

  • Stored only on your device
  • Never transmitted to any server or third party
  • Accessible only by the PaycheckPro app on your device
  • Deleted when you uninstall the app

4. Internet Access

PaycheckPro does not require an internet connection to function. The App does not make any network requests. No data is sent to or received from any external server at any time.

All tax tables and calculation logic are bundled directly within the app and do not require a connection to update.

5. Third-Party Services

PaycheckPro does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, or any similar service.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share your data with any third parties. There is no data to share — all information remains on your device.

7. Financial Disclaimer

PaycheckPro is a paycheck estimation tool only. Calculations are based on published tax tables and are intended as estimates. Results may differ from your actual paystub due to employer-specific withholding methods.

This App does not provide financial, tax, or legal advice. For payroll accuracy, consult your employer's payroll department or a qualified tax professional.

8. Children's Privacy

PaycheckPro is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since the App does not collect any personal information from any users, this is satisfied by design.

9. Security

Since PaycheckPro stores data locally on your device and does not transmit data over the internet, the security of your data is protected by your device's built-in security features (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We recommend keeping your device secured with a PIN, password, or biometric lock.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
